# EXPLANATION STRUCTURE — WEBSITE VOCAB

**Canonical website explanation standard · Version 1.1 (approved 2026-10-21)**

This is the **authoritative editorial structure** for creating, rewriting, reviewing and displaying explanations on the website. When an older generator, template or existing explanation conflicts with this document, **this standard controls new content and quality reviews**. It does not automatically certify or rewrite existing solutions.

**Included:** Vocabulary (all grades and review banks), synonym/antonym, word form, sentence transformation, School Practice, Mid-term, Reading, Listening and **Lesson Plans** (including protected Supabase solutions).

**Temporarily excluded:** Grammar Testing currently under development (including teacher-only Grammar Topic 21). Do not change that module merely to enforce this structure.

## 1. One response, two presentation depths

**Quick Explanation — default, immediately after the existing permission to reveal answers:**

- The actual correct answer (from the existing authoritative answer key).
- **1–2 useful sentences** identifying the strongest specific evidence and the causal/linguistic link to that answer.
- "Xem giải thích chi tiết" to expand the explanation.

**Deep Explanation — expanded, always ordered into three stages:**

**01 · BẰNG CHỨNG — Understand & Identify**

1. Translate or accurately paraphrase the question when this improves understanding; preserve meaning rather than translate word for word.
2. Quote the **specific decisive clue** from the sentence, passage or verified audio. For grammar/word form use the actual structure or position.
3. Explain the **mechanism**: what requirement the clue establishes (meaning, reference, part of speech, tense, collocation, information scope) and why it supports the answer.

Do not repeat an answer as its own evidence. A quoted sentence without reasoning is insufficient; "clue là X" without explaining **why X decides the answer** is insufficient.

**02 · PHÂN TÍCH A–D — Explain & Contrast**

For A–D MCQs, analyze **each actual option A, B, C, D separately**, in source/visible option order:

- The actual meaning/function of the option **in that context**.
- Normal usage, semantic scope, collocation, register, structure, or discourse function **only as relevant**.
- A **concrete, question-specific** mechanism that proves it matches the clue or explains exactly why it fails.
- If two options are both defensible, flag the ambiguity and preserve existing assessment warnings; never invent a reason to reject a valid answer.
- Link the explanation to the option's stable identity; when answers shuffle, analyses and examples must follow their options.

**Do not write** "A phù hợp hơn", "B không phù hợp với ngữ cảnh", "Đây là lý do lựa chọn này khác nghĩa hoặc khác chức năng với X", "Từ này có vẻ hợp vì nằm gần cùng semantic field" **without specifying the actual difference**. If there is no verified option-specific rationale, mark **needs_review**; do not fabricate one.

For non-MCQ tasks, **02 · PHÂN TÍCH CÁCH GIẢI** replaces A–D: explain necessary transformation/derivation, preservation of meaning, grammatical constraints and common error mechanisms. Never fabricate A–D options.

**03 · VẬN DỤNG — Apply & Transfer**

For **Vocabulary A–D**, provide exactly **one original, complete, natural English usage example for each choice A/B/C/D**, with **one accurate Vietnamese translation for each**. The example must demonstrate correct real-world usage of that **particular** word or phrase, not merely reuse the exam question. Avoid fabricated collocations, incomplete sentences and generic examples that do not actually use the option.

For other tasks, adapt the transfer element:
- **Reading:** verified evidence → paraphrase/equivalence → specific trap/transferable comprehension mechanism. For True/False/Not Given, identify which precise proposition is established, contradicted or not determined; absence of a single keyword alone does not prove Not Given.
- **Listening:** verified timestamp and actual spoken words → correction/negation/contrast or equivalent phrase → targeted replay and useful listening pattern. Timestamp alone is **not** a verified transcript.
- **Word Form:** base form → required grammatical role → derived form and pertinent word family; demonstrate in another correct sentence.
- **Sentence Transformation:** meaning invariants and structural change → worked parallel pair → a relevant transformation trap.
- **School/Mid-term:** teach the specific clue, semantic or grammatical contrast, and relevant pattern/example; preserve any warning about faulty exam questions.
- **Lesson Plans:** use **the same three stages and four bilingual examples for Vocabulary A–D**, using the authorized solution payload; do not publish protected answers to a public file.

**Điểm cần nhớ / Takeaway is conditional, not obligatory filler.** Add **only** a specific reusable distinction, collocation, idiom, structure, paraphrase or error pattern that is **not already repeated word-for-word** in the preceding stages. If no worthwhile unique takeaway exists, omit the section entirely.

## 2. Golden example — food waste vs. gender equality

Question: **Buying only the food we need is a simple way to reduce ______ at home.**

A. work-life balance · B. food waste · C. family bonding · D. gender equality

**Quick:** **B. food waste** — "Buying only the food we need" describes buying enough food to avoid surplus that gets thrown away; therefore **reduce food waste** fits the intended result.

**01 · Bằng chứng:** "Buying only the food we need" → chỉ mua đủ lượng cần dùng → giảm đồ ăn thừa phải bỏ đi. The thing that is reduced is wasted food, not a social relationship or a principle of equality.

**02 · Phân tích A–D:**
- **A · work-life balance — Sai:** cân bằng thời gian/đòi hỏi giữa công việc và cuộc sống; câu đang nói về mua và sử dụng thực phẩm, không nói phân bổ thời gian.
- **B · food waste — Đúng:** đồ ăn/thực phẩm bị lãng phí; mua vừa đủ giúp hạn chế lượng thừa phải bỏ đi → khớp quan hệ nhân quả với "reduce".
- **C · family bonding — Sai:** sự gắn kết tình cảm giữa thành viên gia đình, không phải lượng thực phẩm bị bỏ phí. Do **not** classify "bonding" as a verb merely from its -ing ending.
- **D · gender equality — Sai:** bình đẳng quyền/cơ hội giữa các giới; câu không nói về quyền hoặc giới tính.

**03 · Vận dụng (one natural English sentence and Vietnamese translation per actual option):**
- **A:** Flexible hours improve work-life balance. → Giờ làm việc linh hoạt giúp cải thiện sự cân bằng giữa công việc và cuộc sống.
- **B:** Planning meals helps reduce food waste. → Lập kế hoạch bữa ăn giúp giảm lãng phí thực phẩm.
- **C:** Weekend activities are good for family bonding. → Các hoạt động cuối tuần giúp tăng sự gắn kết gia đình.
- **D:** Gender equality means equal opportunities. → Bình đẳng giới nghĩa là cơ hội bình đẳng.

**Optional useful takeaway:** "reduce food waste" = giảm lãng phí thực phẩm; the object of "reduce" here must be something buying only necessary food can actually reduce.

This example is the **editorial quality reference**, not a rigid paragraph template. For each different question, the evidence, contrasts and transferable point must change.

## 3. Quality gates — reject obvious / repetitive explanations

A solution is **not approved** merely because it contains three titled stages, four options or four example fields.

Mandatory checks:

1. **Answer integrity:** Match original answer key and existing scoring; preserve alternatives and ambiguity warnings.
2. **Evidence traceability:** Verify decisive clue in the original question, passage, or actual media. Cite passage location/audio timestamp when available; do not invent evidence.
3. **Complete reasoning:** Explain the intermediate causal, grammatical or semantic link between clue and chosen answer.
4. **Individual distractor discrimination:** For every A–D, identify a **different meaningful reason** tied to that option and the question. Avoid boilerplate and circular statements.
5. **Language correctness:** Check meaning, collocations, word class, semantic scope, example grammar, English usage and Vietnamese translation. Do not infer "noun denoting person" from -er/-or or "verb" from -ing.
6. **Transfer value:** Each example demonstrates the relevant option and each takeaway conveys specific reusable knowledge, not "nhận diện clue", "chú ý từ loại/collocation", or other universal advice.
7. **Non-repetition:** 01 identifies evidence and reasoning; 02 compares choices; 03 demonstrates new uses. Do not restate the same conclusion three times.

Examples of **prohibited filler**:
- "Đây là lý do lựa chọn này khác nghĩa hoặc khác chức năng với 'gender equality' trong chính ngữ cảnh của câu."
- "Khi làm câu tương tự, nhận diện clue trước, sau đó kiểm tra từ loại, cấu trúc, collocation và phạm vi nghĩa."
- "Từ này có vẻ hợp vì nằm gần cùng semantic field, nhưng kém chính xác hơn."
- "B đúng vì phù hợp với ngữ cảnh", "A sai vì không khớp yêu cầu".
- Generic examples such as "The teacher asked us to check the word choice against the whole sentence" as substitutes for actual option usage.

**Never replace unsupported filler with another invented claim.** Remove it, keep grounded analysis, and mark the missing content for editorial review.

## 4. Review and release boundaries

Editorial states: **draft → needs_review → approved**; **blocked** for confirmed serious errors or unsourced/incoherent keys.

- **needs_review**: missing evidence, option-specific analysis, Vocabulary bilingual example(s), source verification, or answer ambiguity. Show the useful verified material without pretending a complete rationale exists.
- **approved**: passes substantive quality checks, not merely non-empty field/shape checks.
- **blocked**: incorrect/contradictory/unsafe answer or critical source error; escalate to editorial review.

**Editorial status is NOT authorization.** Existing authentication, Supabase RLS, teacher unlock and answer-release conditions still govern whether a learner may receive solution data. Lesson Plans remain private until authorized. Do not place answer keys in static public JS or expose them in hidden HTML; print only information authorized for that user.

## 5. Existing implementation and migration

The current presentation component is in:
- \`features/explanation-v1.js\` — shared renderer, adapters, status.
- \`features/explanation-v1.css\` — concise/default + expanded-three-step presentation.
- \`features/lesson-plans.js\` — authorized Lesson Plans solutions, including detailed print.
- \`tests/explanation-v1.test.cjs\`, \`tests/explanation-content-quality.test.cjs\` — partial automated regression coverage.

**The UI is shared, but existing banks are not automatically editorially approved**:
- Grade 9–10 Vocabulary currently has some explanations without per-option A–D analysis or the four bilingual examples.
- Grade 12 generated semantic banks contain question-specific lexical metadata/examples but still require human editorial checks for each distractor.
- Listening has answer/timestamp pointers but not a verified spoken-evidence rationale for each question.
- Some Grade 10 Mid-term items have missing choice-specific explanations and must remain flagged.
- Lesson Plans solutions live in Supabase; the current \`examples\` field is sometimes a string whereas the V1 adapter expects an array. Correct schema mapping and verify access protection before claiming full compatibility.

**Do not mass-edit existing data automatically merely because this document is approved**. When creating/editing a new lesson, generate under this structure, validate factual/evidence quality, then publish only after appropriate review.

## 6. Acceptance criteria for future authoring

A new explanation can be called **Explanation Standard V1.1 compliant** only if it:
- Provides clear short answer and the three stages in the prescribed sequence.
- For Vocabulary A–D: gives **four distinct accurate rationales and four original English + Vietnamese example pairs**.
- Shows a real, independently traceable clue and the reasoning mechanism.
- Avoids repetitive stock advice, tautological phrasing and unsupported claims.
- Omits optional takeaway rather than filling it with a universal tip.
- Preserves answer keys, assessment flow, teacher permissions, student access and print restrictions.
- Passes content checks and applicable browser/regression tests.

**Instruction for future human and AI contributors:** Refer to this document before authoring or revising explanations. Apply the pedagogical rules to the content, **not just the card headings**. Avoid changing the excluded Grammar Testing area without separate approval.
